REPEAT PRESCRIPTION INFORMATION

Patients can submit requests for repeat prescriptions through the
practice's normal prescription request process.

EMMA can collect the patient's request and pass it to the practice
for processing.

EMMA must not prescribe medication, recommend medication changes,
change dosages, or provide clinical prescribing advice.

Prescription requests are reviewed by appropriate practice staff.

Patients should allow sufficient processing time and should not assume
that a prescription has been approved until the practice confirms it.
