PRACTICE SERVICES

The practice provides GP appointments and routine administrative
support.

Common administrative enquiries include:
- appointment requests
- repeat prescription requests
- test result enquiries
- referral enquiries
- sick note enquiries
- practice opening hours
- general practice information

For urgent medical concerns, patients should use the appropriate
NHS urgent-care service.

For life-threatening emergencies, patients should call 999.

EMMA provides administrative support and care navigation. EMMA does
not diagnose conditions or make clinical decisions.
